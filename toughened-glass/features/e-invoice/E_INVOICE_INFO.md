# e-Invoice: Complete Process & Field Guide

> **Target Audience:** Business owners, accounts team, billing operators, and anyone at Rubine Glass who wants to understand how e-Invoicing works for a Final Invoice without government or technical jargon.

---

## 1. What is an e-Invoice?

It is like taking your finished invoice to a government clerk who checks it, stamps it with an official tamper-proof QR code and a 64-character registration number, and hands it back so that your customer and the tax office both know it is genuine.

**e-Invoice** means a Final Invoice is reported to the government's Invoice Registration Portal (IRP) before it is handed to the customer. The IRP replies with a registration number (IRN), an acknowledgement number and date, and a signed QR code. These are printed on the tax invoice that goes with the glass.

### Key Rules:
* 🏢 **Only for registered customers:** e-Invoice is for B2B sales, so the customer (party) must have a valid 15-character GSTIN. A customer without a GSTIN cannot have an e-Invoice.
* 💼 **Automatic at creation:** The e-Invoice is generated by itself the moment a Final Invoice is created and saved. Nobody has to press a button. A declined or cancelled invoice cannot be sent.
* 🛡️ **One live IRN per invoice:** An invoice can never have two active e-Invoices. If one already exists, a second is refused.
* ⏱️ **24 hour cancel window:** The government allows an IRN to be cancelled only within 24 hours of generating it. After that a credit note is the only way to correct it.
* 🧪 **Test mode is not real:** Until Rubine is connected to the live government service, the system runs in MOCK mode. A MOCK IRN looks real but is not registered anywhere, and the printed invoice says so in red under the QR code.

## 2. Key Components

When the government accepts an invoice, three things come back and are printed on the tax invoice:

```text
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        TAX INVOICE                       │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  RUBINE GLASS (P) LTD                        ┌─────────┐ │
│  GST No.: 33AAFCR6930C1ZO                    │  SIGNED │ │
│                                              │  QR CODE│ │
│  Invoice No.   : FI-20261013-111             └─────────┘ │
│  Invoice Date  : 13-10-2026                              │
│  IRN           : 6afb07a149efef43fff0a737b1a0...         │
│  Ack No. / Date: 112610000123456 / 13-10-2026            │
│                                                          │
│  Items, HSN code, quantity (PCS / SQM), rate, amount     │
│  Basic amount, charges, assessable value, SGST, CGST     │
│  Round off, Grand Total                                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **IRN (Invoice Reference Number):** A 64-character code made by the government from your GSTIN, the invoice number, the financial year and the document type. It is unique to this invoice.
2. **Signed QR Code:** A QR code signed by the government. Anyone can scan it to check the invoice is genuine, even without internet.
3. **Ack Number and Date:** The receipt number and time the government gave when it registered the invoice.

## 3. Step-by-Step Process

```text
  [1. Final Invoice     ──►  [2. System checks    ──►  [3. e-Invoice is
      is created]              the data]                 generated]
                                                              │
                                                              ▼
  [6. Hand over the     ◄──  [5. Print or         ◄──  [4. IRN and QR
      glass + invoice]         download PDF]              come back]
```

### Step 1: The Final Invoice is created
Accounts raise the Final Invoice from a completed Work Order and save it. Nothing new has to be typed for e-Invoice.

### Step 2: The system checks the data
Before anything is sent, the system checks the company GSTIN and address, the customer GSTIN, address and pincode, the HSN code of every product, and that the invoice total matches the taxable value plus GST. If something is missing, it says exactly what to fix and where, for example "The party ARTICO RM INTERIORS has no GSTIN. Add the GSTIN on the party's billing address in Parties."

### Step 3: The e-Invoice is generated
The system sends the invoice to the government by itself and waits for the answer. If the data is not complete, the invoice is still saved and a message says what to fix. A failed attempt is kept in the log. After fixing it, open the invoice preview and click **Generate e-Invoice**.

### Step 4: The IRN and QR come back
The IRN, acknowledgement number, date and QR code are saved against the invoice.

### Step 5: Print or download the tax invoice
The Final Invoice preview now shows the tax invoice with the IRN and the QR code, in the layout of the standard Rubine tax invoice. Print and Download PDF use the same layout. The copy label can be changed (Original for Recipient, Duplicate for Transporter, Triplicate for Assessee). If the invoice is missing data the government needs, the preview shows the earlier layout and says what is missing.

### Step 6: Hand over the glass and the invoice
The signed copy travels with the goods.

## 4. Field-by-Field Explanation

---

### 4.1 Your Company (the seller)
* **Legal name:** The company name on the Company profile.
* **GSTIN:** Your 15-character GST number on the Company profile. The first two digits tell the state (33 is Tamil Nadu).
* **Address, city and pincode:** Taken from the company address. The pincode must be 6 digits.
* **Brand name:** RUBINE TUFF, printed in the header of the invoice.

---

### 4.2 The Customer (the buyer)
* **Name:** The party name.
* **GSTIN:** Entered on the party's address in Parties. It is required. The first two digits decide whether the sale is inside Tamil Nadu (CGST + SGST) or outside it (IGST).
* **Billing address:** The party's Billing (or Office) address, or its default address if none is marked.
* **Ship To address:** The party's Delivery (or Shipping) address. If there is none, the billing address is used.

---

### 4.3 Items and Charges
* **Glass items:** Each product on the Final Invoice, with pieces, chargeable square metres, rate and amount. The government quantity is in SQM because glass is priced per square metre.
* **HSN code:** A product code the government requires on every item. Toughened glass uses 70071900. It is kept on the Product master.
* **Charges:** Polish, logo, documentation, energy and handling charges from the Proforma Invoice. Only this invoice's share is used when the order is invoiced in parts. They are listed on the same HSN as the glass because they are part of the same supply.
* **Discount:** The order discount, shared across the lines in proportion to their value.

---

### 4.4 Invoice Header and Totals
* **Invoice number:** The Final Invoice code (for example FI-20261013-111). The government allows at most 16 letters, digits, "/" and "-", and it must not start with 0, "/" or "-".
* **Invoice date:** The Final Invoice date.
* **GST rate:** Rubine does not store GST on the invoice, only the total. The system works the rate back from the total and matches it to a standard rate (for example 18%). If no standard rate fits, it stops and asks for the invoice to be checked.
* **Round off:** The small difference (at most 2 rupees) between the exact total and the whole-rupee Grand Total.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Do I have to press anything to make the e-Invoice?
No. It is made when the Final Invoice is saved. You only press **Generate e-Invoice** in the preview if the automatic attempt failed, for example because a customer GSTIN was missing.

### Q2: I made a mistake after generating. What do I do?
If it is within 24 hours, cancel the e-Invoice, fix the invoice or the master data, and generate again. After 24 hours the government does not allow cancelling, so a credit note has to be raised instead (credit notes are not built yet).

### Q3: Why does it say my customer has no GSTIN?
e-Invoice is only for registered customers. For a customer without a GSTIN you will see this message every time an invoice is saved, and the invoice itself is not affected. Add the GSTIN on the party's billing address in Parties. If the customer really has no GSTIN, no e-Invoice is needed for that sale.

### Q4: Why does it say a product has no HSN code?
Every product on the invoice needs an HSN code. Toughened glass products are filled in automatically (70071900). Other products, such as Annealed Glass, are left empty on purpose because their code is different. Someone who knows the right code must enter it on the Product master.

### Q4b: What if the invoice is declined after the e-Invoice was made?
The e-Invoice stays registered with the government. Cancel it from the invoice within 24 hours, or raise a credit note. This is not automatic yet.

### Q5: The printed invoice says "SIMULATED (MOCK)". Is it valid?
No. It means the system is in test mode and nothing was sent to the government. It becomes a real e-Invoice only after Rubine is connected to the live service by the technical team.

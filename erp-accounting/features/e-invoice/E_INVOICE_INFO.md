# e-Invoice: Complete Process & Field Guide

> **Target Audience:** Business owners, accounts team, billing operators, and anyone who wants to understand how e-Invoicing under Indian GST works without complicated government jargon.

---

## 1. What is an e-Invoice?

Imagine you issue a business invoice to your customer. Before you hand it to them, you take it to a government official who checks the details and puts an **official tamper-proof digital stamp (QR Code)** and a **64-character registration number (IRN)** on top of it.

An **e-Invoice (Electronic Invoice)** is simply an invoice created on your ERP software that is **reported to and authenticated by the Government's Invoice Registration Portal (IRP)** in real-time.

### Key Rules:
* 🏢 **Applicability:** Mandatory for all GST-registered businesses whose annual aggregate turnover exceeds the government threshold (e.g., ₹5 Crore).
* 💼 **Transaction Types:** Required for all **B2B (Business to Business)** sales, **Exports**, **SEZ supplies**, **Credit Notes (CRN)**, and **Debit Notes (DBN)**.
* 🛡️ **Why it exists:** To eliminate manual GST reporting errors, prevent fake invoices, and enable auto-population of GST returns (GSTR-1) and e-Way Bills.

---

## 2. Key Components of an e-Invoice

When an invoice is approved by the Government IRP, three vital elements are added to it:

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   OFFICIAL e-INVOICE                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  AUTHENTICATION DETAILS (Added by Government IRP)       │
│  • IRN: 64-character cryptographic code                 │
│  • Ack Number: 1210 9876 5432                           │
│  • Ack Date: 2026-09-26 14:30:00                        │
│                                           ┌──────────┐  │
│  INVOICE SUMMARY                          │  SIGNED  │  │
│  • Seller GSTIN: 29AAPFU0939F1ZV          │  QR CODE │  │
│  • Buyer GSTIN:  27BBPFU0939F1Z5          │  [  QR  ]│  │
│  • Invoice No:   INV-2026-001               └──────────┘  │
│  • Total Taxable: ₹1,00,000 | Total Tax: ₹18,000            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **IRN (Invoice Reference Number):** A unique 64-character hash code generated by the government using the seller's GSTIN, invoice number, fiscal year, and document type.
2. **Signed QR Code:** A high-density QR code digitally signed by the government. Highway inspectors and buyers can scan it using a mobile app to verify invoice authenticity even without internet.
3. **Ack Number & Date:** The official acknowledgment receipt number and timestamp issued by the IRP system.

## 3. Step-by-Step e-Invoice Process

```text
  [1. Create B2B Invoice]  ──►  [2. Validate Data]  ──►  [3. Send to Gov Portal (IRP)]
                                                                    │
                                                                    ▼
  [6. Share PDF with Buyer] ◄── [5. Print QR & IRN] ◄── [4. Receive IRN & QR Code]
```

### Step 1: Create B2B Invoice
The billing user enters customer details, product items, HSN codes, rates, and GST tax calculations in the ERP.

### Step 2: Validate Data
The system automatically checks that both Seller and Buyer have valid 15-character GSTINs, valid HSN codes, and correct tax math (Intra-state vs Inter-state).

### Step 3: Send to IRP
The ERP sends the invoice JSON payload to the Government IRP portal (or via GSP API).

### Step 4: Receive IRN & Signed QR Code
The IRP verifies the data, generates the 64-character IRN, signs the QR code, records the transaction, and returns the response within milliseconds.

### Step 5: Print QR & IRN on Invoice
The ERP saves the IRN, Ack No, Ack Date, and Signed QR Code to the database and displays them on the official invoice print template.

### Step 6: Share PDF / Auto-Populate Returns
The final invoice is delivered to the customer. The invoice data automatically flows into GSTR-1 returns and can be used to generate an e-Way Bill with one click!

## 4. Field-by-Field Explanation

Here is what every single field in the e-Invoice system means:

---

### 4.1 Seller (Supplier / Consignor) Details
* **Seller Legal Name:** Your registered business name on the GST portal.
* **Seller GSTIN:** Your 15-character statutory GST identification number (e.g., `29AAPFU0939F1ZV`).
* **Seller Address & PIN Code:** Physical address and 6-digit postal code of your registered place of business.
* **Seller State Code:** 2-digit GST state code (e.g., `29` for Karnataka, `27` for Maharashtra).

---

### 4.2 Buyer (Recipient / Consignee) Details
* **Buyer Legal Name:** Customer's registered business name.
* **Buyer GSTIN:** Customer's 15-character GSTIN. Must be present for B2B e-invoices.
* **Billing Address & PIN Code:** Customer's billing address and 6-digit postal code.
* **Place of Supply (POS):** 2-digit state code where goods/services are delivered. Determines whether CGST+SGST or IGST applies.

---

### 4.3 Invoice Header Fields
* **Invoice Number:** Unique document identification number (max 16 characters, allowed symbols: `/` and `-`). Must be unique across the financial year.
* **Invoice Date:** Date when invoice was issued (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
* **Document Type:** 
  * `INV`: Tax Invoice (Standard sale)
  * `CRN`: Credit Note (Issued for returns, discounts, or price reductions)
  * `DBN`: Debit Note (Issued for additional charges or under-billing)
* **Supply Type:**
  * `B2B`: Regular Business-to-Business sale
  * `SEZWP` / `SEZWOP`: Sale to Special Economic Zone (With/Without tax payment)
  * `EXPWP` / `EXPWOP`: Export of goods/services (With/Without tax payment)

---

### 4.4 Line Item Details
* **Product Description:** Name or description of the product/service.
* **HSN / SAC Code:** Harmonized System of Nomenclature (4, 6, or 8 digits) required for goods and services classification.
* **Quantity & Unit (UoM):** Quantity sold and standard GST Unit of Measurement code (e.g., `NOS`, `KGS`, `BOX`, `PCS`, `MTR`).
* **Unit Price:** Selling price per unit before taxes.
* **Discount:** Discount amount subtracted before applying GST.
* **Taxable Value:** Net taxable amount calculated as `(Quantity × Unit Price) - Discount`.

---

### 4.5 Tax Calculation Rules
* **Intra-State Sale (Seller State Code == Buyer State Code):**
  * **CGST:** Central GST (e.g., 9% for an 18% item)
  * **SGST:** State GST (e.g., 9% for an 18% item)
  * **IGST:** Must be ₹0.00
* **Inter-State Sale (Seller State Code != Buyer State Code):**
  * **IGST:** Integrated GST (e.g., 18% full rate)
  * **CGST & SGST:** Must be ₹0.00

---

### 4.6 Key Government Output Fields

* **IRN (Invoice Reference Number):** 64-character unique hex string (e.g., `4a8f9c1b3d...`).
* **Ack Number:** 12 to 15 digit registration number assigned by government.
* **Ack Date:** Official timestamp of registration.
* **Signed QR Code:** Encrypted string containing signed invoice summary for visual verification.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Can I edit an invoice after generating an IRN?
**No.** Once an IRN is generated on the Government portal, the invoice details **cannot be edited or modified**. If there is an error, you must **cancel the e-invoice within 24 hours** and issue a fresh invoice with a new invoice number, or issue a Credit/Debit Note.

### Q2: Is e-Invoicing required for B2C (Business to Consumer) invoices?
**No.** e-Invoicing under GST applies only to **B2B**, **Export**, **SEZ**, and **Credit/Debit Notes**. B2C invoices do not require IRN generation.

### Q3: Can I cancel an e-Invoice anytime?
**No.** An e-Invoice can only be cancelled on the IRP within **24 hours** of generation. After 24 hours, cancellation via IRP is blocked by government rules; you must issue a Credit Note to adjust the transaction in GSTR-1.

### Q4: What is the difference between an e-Invoice and an e-Way Bill?
* 📜 **e-Invoice:** Validates the **sale of goods/services** and records tax data with the government (Generates IRN & Signed QR Code).
* 🚚 **e-Way Bill:** Authorizes the **physical movement/transport** of goods on the road in a vehicle (Generates 12-digit EWB Number & Part B Vehicle tracking).
